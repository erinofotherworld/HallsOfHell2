THIS PAGE IS:
A service and information page for Erin Nyght's tarot 
reading practice. Specifically: Intuitive Psychological 
Tarot Readings framed as shadow work and self-reflection 
tools — not fortune telling, not prediction.
This replaces the former IntPsycRead.html page which 
no longer exists in the project.

VISUAL IDENTITY — PRIESTESS CHAMBER VARIATION:
Same dark gothic base as the rest of the site but with 
a specific quality: ceremonial stillness. Like entering 
a room where something important is about to happen.
  - Section borders: --primary-rose (deeper, more intimate
    than the gold of The Hearthstone)
  - Card imagery: tarot cards from images/tarot/ folder
  - Background: site default (InfernalRealm.jpg via theme.css)
  - Atmosphere: candlelit, suspended, oracular

TONE OF THIS PAGE:
  Serious but not cold. Erin is a practitioner, not 
  a performer. The readings are described as a 
  psychological mirror — they show what is already 
  present, not what will happen. Language is direct,
  warm beneath the formality, and completely honest 
  about what a reading is and is not.
  No fortune telling language anywhere.
  No "your future" or "what will happen."
  Yes to: "what is present," "what is unexamined,"
  "what the cards reflect back."

WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT DO:
  - Take bookings through a form (direct to TheAbyssalGate)
  - Promise outcomes or predictions
  - Use mystical language that obscures rather than clarifies
  - Oversell or use pressure tactics of any kind
