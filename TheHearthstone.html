Things That Shaped This Path

Not a bibliography. Not a reading list. A small collection of specific things — books, moments, ideas, encounters — that cracked something open or pointed in a direction that mattered. In no particular order. Added to as things surface.

Book By Oak Ash & Thorn: Modern Celtic Shamanism, by DJ Connway.

Memory *August, 11996HE - I was on vacation with my mother, father, and siblings in AZ visiting my uncle and his pool. One day I found and got a book on Celtic Shamanism in a small bookstore.

Memory *Early, June 11997HE - I remember going off on another journey to learn how to merge my consciousness with the wood grains of medicine space. As I kept tabs on the compartmentalized Body and the AutoPilot Consciousness that keeps it moving, I was vaguely engaged in its antics other than that it and the mind is safe. I should have known when I lost contact with the autopilot that something was wrong, but I continued my lesson in the journey. When I tried to return to the body and my consciousness violently bounced back into the subconciousness leaving me formless in my own mind - is when my exile from my life began.

Memory *August, 12024HE - This consciousness returns as the host of this body full time after years of a slow crawl back into the conscious mind.
