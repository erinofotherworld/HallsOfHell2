The Qliphothic Tree

The Qliphothic Tree — the Tree of Death, the shadow structure beneath the Kabbalistic Tree of Life — is the primary framework of this practice. Ten spheres, each a quality of primal power, each ruled by a demonic force, each requiring direct confrontation and integration rather than avoidance. The work moves sphere by sphere, from the outermost to the innermost. There is no shortcut through the tree.

Pathworking through the Qliphoth is not invocation for its own sake. It is the systematic dismantling of illusions about the self — the qualities each sphere holds are not external forces to be commanded but internal territories to be mapped and inhabited. Lilith rules the outer sphere. The work begins with her and returns to her.
