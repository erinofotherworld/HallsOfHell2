What The Cards Are...

Tarot is a symbolic language built from 78 images that map the full range of human experience — desire, fear, grief, transformation, stillness, destruction, renewal. What Carl Jung once called the collective unconscious lives in these cards: the archetypes that move beneath every personal story, shaping behaviour before the conscious mind has any say. A tarot reading doesn't introduce anything foreign. It illuminates what is already operating inside you and lays it on the table where you can finally look at it directly.

I am not a Fortune Teller, nor do I use these cards for this reason. this is not prediction, it's not divination in the carnival sense, and not a 1 (800) hotline to any divine authority. The cards do not know the future — no one does, because it has not been written yet. What the cards reflect is what's here now in front of us: the patterns are repeating, the tensions we carry, the questions we keep circling without asking out loud. That is what makes a reading useful rather than merely entertaining. It shows us what is here, now, whether we're were ready to see it or not.

Shadow work is the specific lens through which these readings operate. The shadow — in Jung's framework — is everything we have disowned, suppressed, or refused to look at: the anger we call patience, the grief we call strength, the needs we pretend you do not have. These disowned pieces of ourselves that do not disappear. They drive behaviour from deep underneath our depths, and they will keep driving it until they are acknowledged. A tarot reading structured the way it is - as an invitation to sit across from what usually look away from — not to fix it, not to perform any healing... yet. But to see it clearly and let that clarity do its own form of Magick you wont find in the light.

What Readings With Me Looks Like...

Regardless of the reading being in person or online: video or text, I offer a choice in Dark Tarot in the reading. I have 2 "flavors" of Dark Tarot to choose from. One is colorful, whimsical, full of fantasy. Its more willing to gently pull back the curtain of your mind and in a kind touch, point out whats back there and what needs to be worked on - using a Motherly Tone to it.
The other is FAR darker... its more inclined to rip the curtain down and point out that "You done fucked up; here's how you can unfuck yourself... and if you haven't guessed it yet: Fuck You."
Which would you choose?
I started seeing the Tarot as Psychology and Shadow Work after I had a moment when I questioned my faith. At the time I saw the Tarot as Divination to my Goddesses. But when my faith was questioned and my mind wouldnt stop fighting its self, the cards offered no guidance, no help - no assurances. For days I got no answer until I got cleaver and shifted my question and wondered what makes the Tarot tick? I learned that the images are designed to prompt a response, and the words in the booklet are doubly so... the shuffling of a deck is a combination of non coscious mind controlling the hands micro movements as the querrent feels. Readings stopped being Divination to me. They started being Psychology.
As for the shadow work, I am a woman with many fractures working to piece it back together in some form of cohesion: both with some success and massive drawbacks. I started using the cards for working with the Shadows when I found success using them in relation with some aspects of my Dissociative Identity Disorder. when I leaned into the Shadow work after the Rite Of Sulfur was recited and with Lilith and specific questions: thats when I started really forming the shadow work part of these readings.

Each reading is structured the same with very limited variations to it. I dont offer spreads like a fortune teller because: Im not a fortune teller. What I do offer is 3 parts of a single reading: one at a time - a 3 card: "where we come from, where we are, where we are headed" first as a broad brush stroke generalization of who the person is and their trajectory based on this broad brush stroke. If direction and/or clarification is needed; 3 more cards are offered to give just that - clarification and direction in a simple 3 card "Do this/ Don't do this & an outcome." For many this is where the reading gets real because while the generalization is generalized, its not detailed. This is also where the choice in decks affects the narritave behind the reading. If the clarification is where this reading becomes real for many - imagine the gravity of this reality in a "motherly tone" for just a moment.... now imagine the same with the tone of someone willing to tell you "you fucked up." ... Yea... Thats the difference.
One final tier of the reading is offered if it is needed: the single card read as something to focus on for the rest of the day - because everything else is a lot of information... focus on this cherry on top.
The ONLY variation to this reading I offer comes in how the cards are shuffled. I still hold onto some of my old way, a bit of sentiment... but I still think that if the querrent is shuffling the deck and cards fall out - those cards are messages to the client directly from... ... ... ... ... insert deity, or entity here.
The wonder world of the internet has opened us to an Information Age that really is limitless if you put your mind to it. If you think about it, when COVID-19 & the pandemic shut everything down many of the essential workers who had the flexibility to work remotly - did: and thats what I offer too. I live in a 5th wheel on a mobile data connection mostly. I am a nomad for all intents and purposes, so too is almost everything I do. I work the ren fairs, but I always got my phone with me on a good unlimited data plan.

Im going to go out on a limb and suspect that you took a good look at his site and got a good feeling of who I am before you came close to deciding that you are interested in a reading: excellent! BUT a couple of things must be discussed before booking a session with me.
This is not your fortune teller type reading where you must ask a question and the reader tells you whats coming your way - No. Unlike in person readings, you tell me what you wish to know and I start shuffling the deck with that in mind - ONLY that: and your image in mind, but my mind is blank. Reading Formats

Each format below serves a different kind of question. If you are unsure which suits your situation, say so when you reach out — that conversation is part of the process.

The Single Card

One card drawn in response to one focused question or a single area of life needing illumination. Best for a specific situation, a decision point, or a moment requiring clarity rather than depth.

The Three-Card Draw

Past, present, future — or more accurately: what shaped this, what is present now, what is emerging. Three cards in relationship to each other, read as a narrative rather than in isolation. The most versatile format for general reflection.

The Shadow Spread

A deeper spread designed specifically for shadow work — examining what is being avoided, what has been disowned, and what is asking to be integrated. Not a comfortable reading. An honest one. For those ready to look directly at what is driving them.

The Full Spread

A comprehensive reading covering multiple areas of life or a complex situation requiring full context. Erin's most in-depth offering — suitable for significant crossroads, major transitions, or when a thorough examination of where you are is needed.

Distance readings available worldwide.
All readings conducted with the understanding that the cards reflect — they do not prescribe.
