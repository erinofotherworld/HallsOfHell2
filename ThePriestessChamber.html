What The Cards Are

Tarot is a symbolic language built from 78 images that map the full range of human experience — desire, fear, grief, transformation, stillness, destruction, renewal. What Carl Jung called the collective unconscious lives in these cards: the archetypes that move beneath every personal story, shaping behaviour before the conscious mind has any say. A tarot reading does not introduce anything foreign. It externalises what is already operating inside you and lays it on the table where you can finally look at it directly.

On this page, tarot is not treated as fortune telling. It is not prediction, not divination in the carnival sense, and not a hotline to any divine authority. The cards do not know your future — no one does, because it has not been written yet. What the cards reflect is what is present: the patterns you are repeating, the tensions you are carrying, the questions you keep circling without asking out loud. That is what makes a reading useful rather than merely entertaining. It shows you what is here, now, whether you were ready to see it or not.

Shadow work is the specific lens through which these readings operate. The shadow — in Jung's framework — is everything you have disowned, suppressed, or refused to look at: the anger you call patience, the grief you call strength, the needs you pretend you do not have. These disowned pieces do not disappear. They drive behaviour from underneath, and they will keep driving it until they are acknowledged. A tarot reading structured as shadow work is an invitation to sit across from what you usually look away from — not to fix it, not to perform healing on command, but to see it clearly and let that seeing do its own work.

Reading Formats

Each format below serves a different kind of question. If you are unsure which suits your situation, say so when you reach out — that conversation is part of the process.

The Single Card

One card drawn in response to one focused question or a single area of life needing illumination. Best for a specific situation, a decision point, or a moment requiring clarity rather than depth.

The Three-Card Draw

Past, present, future — or more accurately: what shaped this, what is present now, what is emerging. Three cards in relationship to each other, read as a narrative rather than in isolation. The most versatile format for general reflection.

The Shadow Spread

A deeper spread designed specifically for shadow work — examining what is being avoided, what has been disowned, and what is asking to be integrated. Not a comfortable reading. An honest one. For those ready to look directly at what is driving them.

The Full Spread

A comprehensive reading covering multiple areas of life or a complex situation requiring full context. Erin's most in-depth offering — suitable for significant crossroads, major transitions, or when a thorough examination of where you are is needed.

Distance readings available worldwide.
All readings conducted with the understanding that the cards reflect — they do not prescribe.
