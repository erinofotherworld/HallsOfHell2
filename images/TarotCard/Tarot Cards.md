# Major Arcana: Shadow Work & Psychology

This repository contains `.jpg` assets of the **Major Arcana**, curated for use in **Tarot for Psychology** and **Shadow Work** practices.

---

## 📂 Contents
* **22 High-Resolution Images:** The complete Major Arcana (0–XXI).
* **Focus:** Visual archetypes designed to act as "mirrors" for the subconscious.

## 🧠 Application
These assets are intended for practitioners using Tarot as a tool for:
* **Archetypal Mapping:** Visualizing the "Fool's Journey" as a path of psychological growth.
* **Shadow Integration:** Identifying repressed traits through the "darker" imagery of the cards.
* **Digital Altars:** Creating private, secure spaces for emotional reflection and "energy fuel" processing.

## 🛠 Usage
1. **Selection:** Use cards as focal points for journaling or active imagination.
2. **Analysis:** Focus on cards that trigger strong emotional responses to uncover hidden internal narratives.
3. **Integration:** Use the symbolic language of the cards to bridge the gap between the conscious and unconscious mind.
